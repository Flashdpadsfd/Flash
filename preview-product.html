Common questions
How fast is delivery?

Seconds, in most cases. If your payment is still confirming on the network it can take a few minutes — the checkout page keeps you posted.

Where do I find what I bought?

Sign in with the email you ordered with and open the order. Everything we sent stays there, so you can come back to it later.

What if it stops working?

Can I change the password?

No. Changing it breaks the account and voids the replacement — leave it exactly as delivered.
